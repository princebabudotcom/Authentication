## How to Practice & Log

| Activity                                 | Where to note                                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Problem name, link, difficulty           | Digital (spreadsheet/notes app)                           |
| Approach/pattern explanation (own words) | Physical diary                                            |
| Time & space complexity                  | Digital                                                   |
| Mistakes/edge cases missed               | Digital — "mistake log"                                   |
| Brute force → optimized thought process  | Physical diary (helps in interviews to explain reasoning) |

**Tip:** For company-wise lists, prioritize by actual interview experience reports (LeetCode company tags, GFG interview experiences) closer to your interview date — patterns shift over time.
